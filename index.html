03 Features

Multi-format

Reads QR codes plus common barcodes: EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39, ITF and more.

Three ways to scan

Live camera, a photo from your phone's own camera app (zoom/macro included), or an existing image.

Smart actions

Links open or copy in one tap; barcodes get a "Search Online" lookup; Wi-Fi, email, phone and contact cards get matching quick actions.

100% local & private

Flashlight support

Toggle your device torch for scanning in low light, where supported.

Scan history

Your recent scans stay saved on this device so you can revisit, copy or delete them anytime.

04 How To Use

01

Pick a mode

"Live Camera" scans in real time. "Take Photo" opens your phone's own camera app — handy when a code needs zoom or macro focus. "Upload Image" reads a code from a saved picture.

02

Grant camera access (Live Camera only)

Tap "Enable Camera" and accept the browser's permission prompt. This must be done manually once per session — browsers won't start the camera without it.

03

Align the code

Hold the QR code or barcode inside the bracket guides, filling most of the frame, and keep your hand steady until it locks on.

04

Read the result

The content type shows as a badge, with matching actions underneath — open or copy a link, search a barcode online, call a number, save a contact, and more.

05

Revisit anytime

Every scan is logged under "Recent Scans" on this device, so you can copy or reopen it later without rescanning.

For your safety, links do not open automatically by default — review the address first, then tap "Open Link". Turn on "Auto-open links" only for sources you fully trust.
