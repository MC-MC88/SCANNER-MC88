Camera or photo

Smart actions

100% local & private

04 How To Use

01

Choose a mode

Pick "Camera" to scan live, or "Upload Image" to decode a photo already saved on your phone.

02

Grant camera access

Tap "Start Camera" and allow the browser permission prompt. Your browser only sends video to this page — never to a server.

03

Align the code

Hold the QR code or barcode inside the bracket guides, roughly filling the frame, and keep your hand steady.

04

Read the result

Once decoded, the content type is shown as a badge, and matching quick actions appear — open a link, call a number, save a contact, and more.

05

Revisit anytime

Every scan is logged under "Recent Scans" on this device, so you can copy or reopen it later without rescanning.

For your safety, links do not open automatically by default — review the address first, then tap "Open Link". Turn on "Auto-open links" only for sources you fully trust.
